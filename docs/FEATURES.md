# Easy Stay — Feature List (Firebase, web + mobile)

Phone OTP is removed. Tags: **[Launch]** ships by 1 January; **[Later]** follows after launch.

## 1. Login and accounts
- [Launch] Google sign-in
- [Launch] Email + password sign-in, with forgot password
- [Launch] Sign in with Apple (iPhone app)
- [Launch] Guest browsing, with login required only to send an enquiry
- [Launch] Email verification before enquiring or listing
- [Launch] Roles: seeker, owner, caretaker, admin
- [Later] Passwordless email-link login
- [Later] Phone number confirmation through WhatsApp

## 2. Seeker app

### Search and discovery
- [Launch] Map and list view of nearby rooms (GPS)
- [Launch] Filters: price, type (PG, flat, hotel, home), gender, sharing, furnishing, food
- [Launch] Women-First filter (female caretaker, guard, well-lit road)
- [Launch] Sort by distance, price or freshness
- [Later] Time-to-Desk commute search (office address + arrival time)
- [Later] Metro and auto stand layer on the map
- [Later] AI smart search ("girls PG near Cyber City under 12k")

### Room details
- [Launch] Verified photo gallery
- [Launch] Rent, deposit, amenities and house rules
- [Launch] "Updated X days ago" freshness label
- [Launch] Live available or sold-out status
- [Launch] Safety Badge: health score and NOC expiry dates
- [Later] True total monthly cost (rent + electricity + food + Wi-Fi + maintenance)
- [Later] Pulse: "X people viewing now"
- [Later] Video tour, sunlight and noise score
- [Later] Verified tenant reviews

### Actions
- [Launch] Send enquiry
- [Launch] Book a visit time slot
- [Launch] Chat with owner (phone numbers hidden until the owner accepts)
- [Launch] Save rooms
- [Launch] Report a listing
- [Launch] SOS button during visits (shares live location)
- [Later] Saved-area alerts for new listings
- [Later] "Notify me when available" waitlist
- [Later] Token payment to lock a room
- [Later] Roommate matcher
